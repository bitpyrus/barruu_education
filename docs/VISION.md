# Vision

**Barruu is an open-source alternative to Brilliant.org for science and engineering education.**

## Who it's for

Learners in **grades 9–12** (ages ~14–18) studying mathematics, physics, chemistry, engineering and computer
science — in school, at home, or self-directed — anywhere in the world. Designed to extend later down to grades 6–8
and up to first-year university by adding skills and courses, not by redesigning.

## How we follow Brilliant — and where we differ

Brilliant (researched Nov 2026) is US-based, English-only, organised by subject/topic with US grade framing
("grade 5 to college"), and leads with interactive problems plus an AI tutor (Koji). Its lessons require an
internet connection. We keep what works and change what limits learners:

| | Brilliant | Barruu |
|---|---|---|
| Organisation | Topics + US grade framing | Same: subjects → courses → chapters → lessons, grade bands 9–12 |
| Learning style | Interactive problems, visual | Same, with **construction-based** answers checked exactly |
| Languages | English | **Multilingual** from day one, incl. right-to-left scripts |
| Where to start | Asks about experience | **Adaptive placement test** on a prerequisite skill map |
| Connectivity | Online only (per its help centre) | **Local-first, offline** in the browser; desktop app planned |
| AI | AI tutor central | AI optional for learners; AI **accelerates creation and translation** |
| Source | Proprietary | **Open source** (planned; private until the owner decides) |
| Subjects | Math, CS, AI, some science | **Science & engineering** focus: math, physics, chemistry, engineering, CS |

## Product principles

1. Learning by doing: the interaction carries the concept.
2. Exact, kind feedback: describe what the learner's answer does; mistakes are "not yet".
3. Meet learners where they are: level detection, prerequisites, their language.
4. Works everywhere: low-end devices, poor connectivity, offline.
5. Trustworthy: deterministic checking, transparent progress, privacy by default (minors).

## Success

A learner opens Barruu in their language, finds their level in a few minutes, learns something real by
manipulating it, and keeps going offline — and educators/contributors can add high-quality interactive courses
quickly with AI assistance and automated validation.
